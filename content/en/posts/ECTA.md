I’ll be presenting this paper at ECTA 2026 in Angers, France, on October 28–30. It looks at how different starting solutions affect Estimation of Distribution Algorithms (EDAs) for the Traveling Tournament Problem.

<!--more-->

We compared feasible, partially feasible, and infeasible initial populations using a univariate EDA and a tree-based EDA. We also studied the effects of penalty strength and local search, our results showed that initialization had more influence on the search than penalty strength.
